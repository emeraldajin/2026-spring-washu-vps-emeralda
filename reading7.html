Reading

“Do Artifacts Have Ethics?”

(By L. M. Sacasas)

Review

Sacasas’s commentary about the morality and ethics of technology ties in directly with the usage of AI. Many of the questions listed work well for determining whether or not AI usage is appropriate for different contexts and tasks. For instance, one big issue I see with ChatGPT users is that they let the AI make them into a person who doesn’t think for themselves and believes anything they see (Question #1). Taking shortcuts using AI can also cultivate the practice of constantly cutting corners and not actually doing the thinking for themselves (Question 2 and 7). The questions also invite reflection and introspection about the way that we use technology. However, I believe that technology is not always morally neutral. AI technology that uses other people’s work without permission to generate work would be inherently unethical regardless of how it was used because of how it was sourced and trained.
