Writing Exercise 1

What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

I think doing something the slow/hard way really helps when I’m trying to learn the process instead of getting a finished product. When I’m making flashcards or taking notes, I could just jot down the concepts I know will be on the exam, but if I want to really know about the concept I’m learning about, I’ll continue taking notes on lectures even after exams are over and the information won’t be used again.

When I want to write an essay, I want to do it by my own means, even if the professor permits some level of AI involvement. Writing essays allows me to explore and put together my thoughts, and I can’t do that if I have a machine spitting and stringing together those thoughts for me, even if I had some minimal input. I want to experience of presenting a thought and developing it as I continue writing the essay to reach my own epiphany, not a machine-made one.

Taking the slow path is also about experiencing. It’s not always about point A and point B. Sometimes I’ll walk through the longer path near the Butterfly garden so I can see pretty flowers, even if cutting through the Danforth University Center is faster. On a different occasion, I walked back from the Loop instead of taking the shuttle because the weather was nice and the streets smelled of spring rain.

I think if we’re always focused on efficiency and speed, we’ll lose value in our own learning, thinking, and experiencing. Amateurs don't do things efficiently, but if we never let ourselves be amateurs or inefficient, we'll never be anything. Back
