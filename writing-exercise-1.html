Writing Exercise 1

What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

I call my in-state best friend once a week, usually on Friday nights, to catch up and hear each other’s voices again. It helps us feel like we’re still connected even when we’re apart and provides a nice outlet to share a part of our lives together. Talking to her can also make me feel more relieved by giving me a break outside of academics. The aspect of being able to share a part of my world with someone I care about is valuable to me. I think it shows that sometimes all someone needs is some company and human connection.
