Writing Exercise 4

What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?

I miss the family vacations I used to have as a kid. I long for a time where I had no worries and my only concern was how to entertain myself on the trip there, where everyone had time for each other, and responsibilities weren’t burdens. Even though I hated the long car rides and the seemingly eternal waiting, there’s something serene about not having to do anything. I miss looking outside the car window and pretending some made-up creature was running alongside it. Kid me had an imagination I lost a lot of. I guess I just associate the family vacations with the past, when things felt simpler, because even if I did go on a family vacation now, I don’t think I want to go anymore. There’s too many rifts that have grown between us, and I just don’t feel willing to.
