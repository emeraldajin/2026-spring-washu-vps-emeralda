Writing Exercise 4

What is something you appreciate or long for from the past? Why?

I miss recess in elementary school, or more specifically, my life before phones became a big thing. It’s not just about having time to play and relax, but also about a time where I could entertain and exist with myself without technology. I fondly remember picking flowers near the outskirts of the woods, eating honeysuckles, finding caterpillars to carry around all recess, listening to the sound of sticks snapping underneath my feet, and piling wood chips on the playground. Even back at home, the way I played was to go to the pine woods, pick up pine cones, and climb all the way up to the steep slopes and pretend I was trying to survive on my own. Without having a device to turn to, my mind was reserved exclusively for my imagination. I feel like we’ve lost the ability to think and to be alone by ourselves. It’s impossible to get away from screens, especially when all sorts of work is done on it and most if not all communication relies on it. I miss entertaining myself with drawing on every surface available and putting together makeshift storybooks. I miss myself.
