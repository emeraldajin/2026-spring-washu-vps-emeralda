Writing Exercise 2

What is a ritual, practice, or routine in your life that is important for your psychological wellbeing and/or fulfillment? Why?

I think the paper and pen(cil) bring out the best in me because it presents limitless possibilities of creation and an outlet for my thoughts. It allows me to transfer my thinking from my mind to the physical world in a non-distracting manner that digital devices cannot. It doesn’t depend on internet connection, electricity, or battery levels. The periphery around it is quiet and I can focus on the paper in front of me. I can draw and express myself freely. Even if erasing and deleting things takes more effort, I feel like I can be more meticulous with the work that I’m doing on paper. Drawing digitally is fun, but there’s a kind of pressure to make something sophisticated and clean, while paper feels more forgiving and messiness feels natural.
